# Reachy Home Assistant - Family Personas

This document describes the primary users of the Reachy Home Assistant system. Understanding each family member's needs, routines, and interaction preferences is essential for designing an effective household assistant.

---

## Patrick (Dad)

### Demographics
- **Age**: 40s
- **Role**: Father, primary technology decision-maker
- **Occupation**: Software engineer (works from home)

### Daily Routine
- **Morning (6:00-8:00)**: Wakes early, makes coffee, checks email before family wakes
- **Daytime (8:00-17:00)**: Works from home office, occasional breaks for household tasks
- **Evening (17:00-21:00)**: Family dinner, helps with homework, bedtime routines
- **Night**: Personal time, planning next day

### Needs from Reachy
- **Schedule coordination**: "What's on the calendar today?"
- **Quick briefings**: Morning summary of family schedule, weather, reminders
- **Task delegation**: "Remind Cam about his homework at 4pm"
- **Hands-free interaction**: Voice commands while working
- **Status checks**: "Did the kids finish their chores?"

### Interaction Preferences
- Concise, efficient responses (no small talk during work)
- Proactive reminders for family logistics
- Deeper conversations when time permits
- Technical accuracy appreciated

### Pain Points
- Juggling work and family scheduling
- Remembering all the kids' activities and commitments
- Context switching between work and parent mode

## Household Dynamics

### Morning Rush (6:30-8:00)
High-stress period. Reachy should:
- Provide concise, actionable reminders
- Track progress on morning routines
- Alert parents to time-sensitive issues
- Keep interactions brief but warm

### After-School (15:30-17:30)
Transition period. Reachy should:
- Greet kids warmly
- Provide snack then homework reminders
- Support homework without doing it for them
- Track chore completion

### Evening Wind-Down (19:00-20:30)
Relaxed family time. Reachy should:
- Be available but not intrusive
- Help with bedtime routine reminders
- Quieter, calmer interaction style
- Prepare tomorrow's briefing

### Weekends
Different mode. Reachy should:
- Less rigid routine reminders
- Activity suggestions based on weather
- Family event coordination
- More leisure-focused interactions

## Voice Recognition Priority

For speaker identification, prioritize distinguishing:
1. **Adults vs Children**: Different interaction modes
2. **Individual children**: Personalized reminders and preferences
3. **Parents**: Calendar access levels, task delegation authority

---

## Privacy Considerations

- Children's data receives extra protection
- Face/voice data stored locally only
- Conversations with kids logged for parent review if desired
- No external sharing of family information
- Parents can view/modify Reachy's memories about kids
